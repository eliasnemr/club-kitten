# Kitten Hatchery support

Thanks for playing! If something isn't working, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with:

- your iPhone model and iOS version
- what you were doing when the problem happened

## Common questions

**How do I get more eggs?** Win battles (tough rivals drop eggs most often), score well in minigames, go on playdates, or buy a mystery or golden egg in the Hatchery.

**My incubator is full. Did I lose an egg?** No. Extra eggs wait in the egg nest for 24 hours and move into the incubator when a slot frees up.

**How do I earn coins?** Minigames, battles and the three daily challenges in the Play tab. Claim all three challenges for a daily bonus that grows with your streak.

**Will my progress transfer to a new phone?** Progress is stored on your device and moves with an iPhone backup or Quick Start transfer.
