# Club Kitten privacy policy

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Club Kitten does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- Your cats, eggs, coins and lounge are saved only on your device.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The in-game friends (Sam, Mia and Leo) are characters in the game, not other players.
- Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

If a future version adds online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will explain what is shared.
