# Club Kitten privacy policy

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Club Kitten keeps the information it handles to the minimum needed to save your game.

## What we store

**Version 1.1 and later: cloud save.** To back up your progress and restore it if you reinstall the app, Club Kitten creates an anonymous account for your install. No name, email or password is asked for. We store:

- **An account ID:** a random identifier for your anonymous account.
- **Your saved game:** your cats, eggs, coins, lounge, furniture, challenges and similar game progress.

This data is used only to save and restore your game. It is not used for advertising, it is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and it is not sold or shared with data brokers.

Your saved game is stored with our database provider, [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).

**Version 1.0** stores everything only on your device and sends nothing.

## What we don't collect

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no contacts, no location and no chat with other players. The in-game friends (Sam, Mia and Leo) are characters in the game, not other people.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the game from your device. To delete your cloud save as well, email us at eliasnemr@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that handle more data, such as online play with friends, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Questions: eliasnemr@gmail.com
