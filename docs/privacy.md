# Club Kitten privacy policy

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Club Kitten keeps the information it handles to the minimum needed to save your game and play with friends.

## What we store

When you play, Club Kitten creates an anonymous account for your install. No email or password is asked for. We store:

- **An account ID:** a random identifier for your anonymous account.
- **Your saved game:** your cats, eggs, coins, lounge, furniture, challenges and similar progress, so it can be backed up and restored.

**Version 1.2 and later: playing with friends.** If you're signed in to Game Center, we also store:

- **Your Game Center player ID and display name,** to recognise you and show your name to your friends. Your Game Center friends who also play Club Kitten are added as friends automatically.
- **What your friends can see:** your display name, friend code, lounge and up to five of your cats, plus guestbook stickers and preset chat phrases you send them. Chat uses a fixed list of phrases; there is no free-text chat.
- **Reports and blocks** you make, so we can keep the game safe.

This data is used only to run the game. It is not used for advertising, it is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and it is not sold or shared with data brokers.

Your data is stored with our database provider, [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).

## Safety

You can report or block any player from the Friends list, their lounge, your guestbook or a playdate invite. Blocked players can't see you, visit your lounge, sign your guestbook or invite you. We review reports and can suspend players who break the rules.

## What we don't collect

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no contacts, no location and no free-text chat.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the game from your device. To delete your account and everything stored with it, open **Den → Settings (gear) → Delete account and data**. It's deleted from our server straight away. You can also email us at eliasnemr@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

## Changes

If a future version handles more data, we will update this policy before that version is released.
