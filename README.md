# Club Kitten

A cozy SwiftUI iPhone game: hatch kittens from mystery eggs, raise them from Kitten to Legend, battle, play minigames and decorate your Cat Lounge. Art and idle animations were made with Higgsfield.

- **Requirements:** Xcode 26, iOS 17+, iPhone
- **Run:** open `KittenHatchery.xcodeproj`, pick your team under Signing & Capabilities, and run
- **Release:** listing text, screenshots and review notes are in [`AppStore/`](AppStore/metadata.md); privacy and support pages are in [`docs/`](docs/)
- **What's next:** [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Online multiplayer (Supabase + Game Center) is built but paused; see [MULTIPLAYER.md](MULTIPLAYER.md)

Debug builds accept launch arguments for testing, such as `-demo` (sample save) and `-tab lounge`; none of them exist in Release builds.
