## Description
Crack open mystery eggs and meet your new best friend. Every egg hatches a kitten with its own breed, personality and stats, from cheeky Tuxedos and chatty Siamese all the way to the legendary British Shorthair and Scottish Fold.

RAISE YOUR CATS
• Watch them grow from Kitten to Young, Adult and finally Legend
• Train stats and unlock moves like Pounce, Hiss, Purr heal and Zoomies
• Fill your Breed book: 10 breeds from Basic to Legendary

PLAY TOGETHER
• Four quick minigames: Yarn chase, Fish toss, Laser dot and Purr rhythm
• Friendly turn-based battles against rival cats
• Daily challenges with coin rewards and streak bonuses

MAKE A HOME
• Decorate your Cat Lounge with furniture from the Cat Mart
• Royal thrones, box castles, fireplaces, fairy lights and more
• Preview everything in your lounge before you buy
• Recolour fabrics, pick wallpaper and name your lounge
• Invite in-game pals over for playdates and find eggs together

No ads. No accounts. Just cats.
